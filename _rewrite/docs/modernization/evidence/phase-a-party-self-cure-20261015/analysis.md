## Implementable continuation

The original ordinary-cast and action-item finish paths support a living PC curing itself while in a party. The concrete native gaps are the blanket `request.party` rejection in PostgreSQLMapService::FinishSkill and `cluster.party` rejection in ReadActItemSkill. Party membership itself does not change the admitted pure HP/MP cure effect, rank, actual equipment/stat calculation, cost, random cure rows or personal recovery/activity clocks. Removing these barriers does not authorize another target or an unsupported group effect.

The request's party WORD is parsed and retained in the existing finish receipt but has **no authority role** in this bounded effect. It is not replaced with zero, copied into cluster state or treated as a membership assertion. A new equality test against a guessed current/effective party ID would introduce a source-incompatible refusal, especially after a loot-mode change to SOLO. Current World membership and party-bar distribution retain their independently authorized process-local contract.

No new schema, wire field, cast nonce, party SQL table or source packet is required. Existing071 receipt origin,072 complete guild-skill registry publication, item/cast/finish checkpoint authority and their unexecuted deployment prerequisites remain unchanged. This slice retains the previous positive own-PC effect boundaries; it does not complete general party combat, group healing, corps, guild skills, unknown effects, full roster recovery or original World admission.

## Actual client party ID and the SOLO trap

Complete `Client/TClient/TClientGame.cpp::GetPartyID:22821–22831` resolves recall/self host when necessary and returns the PC's **raw m_wPartyID**. It does not inspect loot type. `FinishSkill:28759` filters missing/ineligible targets without reordering and invokes `CTClientSession::SendCS_FINISHSKILL_ACK` (`CSSender.cpp:2980`), which writes that raw main-character party WORD. The wire has no party generation or client cast nonce.

Client OnCS_PARTYJOIN_ACK4937 and OnCS_PARTYATTR_ACK5021 write party/chief/commander from the source packets. Map source/native JOIN and ATTR mask these fields to0 when party type is PT_SOLO1. However complete OnCS_CHGPARTYTYPE_ACK5086 changes only m_bPartyItemType; it does not clear or rewrite m_wPartyID. After an ordinary party changes to SOLO without another ATTR, the client can legitimately send its previous nonzero party WORD while server `CTObjBase::GetPartyID` masks effective membership to0. A client that entered through a SOLO JOIN/ATTR can instead send0. Both are real source sequences.

Therefore matching supplied WORD to raw Map party or to effective server party is unnecessary and can reject valid input. The ordinary parser continues reading the field; the complete immutable finish request remains recorded as received. Only the bounded own-PC cure can treat it as inert. Damage, party-target selection or reward attribution cannot borrow this reasoning.

## Local snapshot, membership changes and handoff

Game dispatch holds an actor operation through its offloaded transaction, cache publication and output. BeginPartyProjection (`session_registry.h:210`) requires operations0 and therefore cannot overwrite the actor's Map party tuple during that operation. It times out by closing the client if a real membership transition cannot be projected. The tuple is the World-supplied raw party, type, chief and commander plus hydrated state; it is a **local projection proof**, not durable membership or a new generation counter.

World join/leave/type/chief/status operations serialize on party_operations. OnCheckMainAck at handlers_conn497 also acquires that gate before changing main/handoff state and releases it before cleanup that could acquire the gate recursively. Thus a World party fanout does not cross a newly initiated main transition unnoticed. Close/logout and current-peer checks can still invalidate an operation across awaited work; no send is treated as acknowledged or replayed.

Map handoff has a distinct boundary: BeginTransfer can set TransferOut while another gameplay operation is still active. OnMWReleaseMainReq in handlers_main_transfer.cpp waits for other operations to drain **before** capturing the actual cache and preparing the durable transfer. A cure may finish its transaction and cache write during that wait; its output Ready fence then drops obsolete projections, and transfer capture includes the new committed HP/MP. It would be inaccurate to claim that the gameplay lease prevents handoff from starting. Normalized/full-graph checkpoint writers and original transfer fields remain unchanged.

A concurrent World membership decision may already have committed while the corresponding Map projection waits on this local operation. That does not authorize a different effect target or change the personal cure result. Existing World status reconciliation may reject/delay the old local party's bar update. Lost or busy/stale output remains an explicit no-replay boundary; this slice does not invent full-roster recovery, a fake self DEL, a new membership ACK or a compensation heal.
